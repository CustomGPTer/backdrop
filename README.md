# Backdrop

Backdrop is a free wallpaper changer for Android with privacy controls. Choose 1 to 25 of your own photos per set, frame each one as you add it (crop, straighten, rotate, or fit a landscape photo over a blurred fill), and a new one fades in every time you unlock. It has Safe Mode, optional lock screen changes, a widget, quick settings tiles, an app lock and password-protected backups. It has no adverts, no accounts and no paid version, and it cannot go online.
