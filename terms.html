Your photos

Only use photos you have the right to use. A wallpaper can be seen by anyone who looks at your phone, so think about who might see a photo before you add it, especially photos of other people.

Safe Mode and the app lock

Safe Mode, home-screen-only photos and the app lock reduce the chance of someone seeing your photos. They cannot stop someone who has your unlocked phone, and the lock screen is only covered when Backdrop is set to change it or its live wallpaper is on both screens.

Timing

Android can hold back timers to save battery, so changes and Safe Mode hours may happen a few minutes late unless you allow exact timing. Do not rely on Backdrop for anything where timing matters.
