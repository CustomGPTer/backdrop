Backdrop 1.0, the first release.

A private wallpaper changer for Android. Choose 1 to 25 of your own photos per set, frame each one as you add it (crop, straighten, rotate, or fit a landscape photo over a blurred fill), and a new one fades in every time you unlock. Safe Mode, optional lock screen changes, a widget, quick settings tiles, an app lock and password-protected backups. No adverts, no account, no internet access.

Backdrop.apk, 2.5 MB. Android 8 or later.
