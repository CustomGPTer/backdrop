Backdrop 1.1

Fixed: on Android 13 and later, Live mode could switch itself off after the first change, so the wallpaper stopped changing when you unlocked. Backdrop now keeps track of its own live wallpaper and never replaces it, and notices unlocks two ways. Also: very large photos are added safely, Classic mode takes effect straight away, and Settings shows the battery setting correctly.

Install over 1.0; your photos and settings are kept. If Backdrop shows "One more tap", tap Turn on Backdrop, then Set wallpaper.

Backdrop.apk, 2.5 MB. Android 8 or later.
